# Thesis Submission Guidelines

Based on:

- `writing/resources/manual_-_preparation_of_theses_and_dissertations_2025-26_v4.pdf`
- `writing/resources/thesis_traditional_format_template(2).docx`

This file summarizes the core rules to follow when writing a thesis in the Traditional Format template for submission.

## Core formatting rules

- Use the current `2025-2026` Graduate School manual and template, not older theses as models.
- Confirm with your chair or program director that you are using the `Traditional Format`.
- Use a consistent `12-point` font throughout the document.
- Accepted fonts: `Arial`, `Calibri`, `Courier`, `Garamond`, `Times New Roman`.
- Use `1-inch margins` on all sides.
- Double-space all general text.
- Left-justify the text; do not fully justify the right margin.
- Use Arabic page numbers throughout.
- Do not print page numbers on:
    - `Title Page`
    - `Approval Page`
- Center printed page numbers `0.5 inch` from the bottom of the page.
- Start every major section on a new page.

## Required document order

1. Title Page
2. Approval Page
3. Dedication (optional)
4. Acknowledgments (optional)
5. Preface (optional)
6. Abstract
7. Table of Contents
8. List of Tables (if applicable)
9. List of Figures (if applicable)
10. Key to Symbols (if applicable)
11. Abbreviations (if applicable)
12. Chapter 1 - Introduction
13. Chapter 2 - Review of Literature
14. Chapter 3 - Methodology
15. Chapter 4 - Results
16. Chapter 5 - Discussion and Conclusions
17. Bibliography/References
18. Appendices (if applicable) (Not needed)
19. Vita/Biosketch (optional) (Not needed)

## Front matter rules

### Title page

- Title must be fewer than `200 characters`.
- Use your full legal name.
- Use the month and year of graduation.
- Page counts as page `1`, but the page number does not appear.

### Approval page

- Use the official approval page format.
- The attached template assumes a `3-member` thesis committee.
- If your committee has additional members, get a revised template from the Graduate School.
- Page counts as page `2`, but the page number does not appear.

### Dedication

- Optional.
- Maximum `1 page`.

### Acknowledgments

- Optional.
- Maximum `1 page`.

### Preface

- Optional.
- Maximum `3 pages`.
- It is not a substitute for the Introduction.

### Abstract

- Required.
- Maximum `350 words`.
- Should include:
    - the problem or area of investigation
    - methods and procedures
    - findings
    - conclusions or recommendations

## Table of contents rules

- Titles in the table of contents must match the wording in the body exactly.
- Include at least primary and secondary subdivisions.
- Single-space within each entry.
- Double-space between entries.
- Use leader dots before page numbers.
- Verify that TOC page numbers match the final document exactly.

## Chapter structure in the Traditional Format template

### Chapter 1 - Introduction

- Introduction
- Purpose
- Scope
- Assumptions
- Hypothesis or Research Question
- Significance
- Definitions (if applicable)

### Chapter 2 - Review of Literature

- Prior research
- Theoretical or conceptual framing
- Gap your study addresses

### Chapter 3 - Methodology

- Research design
- Participants, data, or materials
- Procedures
- Instruments
- Analysis methods

### Chapter 4 - Results

- Present findings clearly
- Use tables and figures as needed
- Focus on results rather than interpretation

### Chapter 5 - Discussion and Conclusions

- Interpret results
- State implications
- Note limitations
- Present conclusions and recommendations

## References, tables, figures, and appendices

- Follow your program or discipline style guide consistently unless it conflicts with the Graduate School manual.
- If you use tables, include a `List of Tables`.
- If you use figures, include a `List of Figures`.
- If you use symbols or abbreviations, include the corresponding front matter section.
- Appendices follow the same margin and pagination rules as the rest of the thesis.
- Center appendix titles at the top of the page.
- Any appendix should be referenced at least once in the main text.

## AI, copyright, and research compliance

### AI use

- If you used AI tools such as `ChatGPT`, you must disclose that use in the thesis.
- Describe how the tool was used, typically in the `Methods` section or a comparable section.
- Include the prompt used and cite the tool in the references.
- Long AI outputs can be placed in an appendix.

### Research compliance

- Complete Responsible Conduct of Research requirements before starting research activity.
- If your research involves human subjects, obtain `IRB` approval before recruitment or data collection.
- If your research involves vertebrate animals, obtain `IACUC` approval before data collection.

### Copyright and plagiarism

- Cite all sources properly.
- If you include copyrighted material beyond fair use, obtain permission.
- Include evidence of permission in an appendix when required.

## Defense and submission deadlines

- The thesis must be publicly defended.
- The defense must be successfully completed `3 weeks before the last day of finals week` in the semester you intend to graduate.
- Final submission requires:
    - the signed approval form
    - the completed thesis in `PDF` format
    - submission by the `last day of the semester`
- Missing the deadline may require enrollment in continuous enrollment credits.
- After Graduate School approval, submit the final approved thesis to `ScholarWorks@GVSU`, unless an exemption or embargo is approved.

## Template usage checklist

- Replace all placeholder text in brackets.
- Keep the section order from the template.
- Remove optional pages only if you will not use them.
- Update the table of contents after headings and page numbering are final.
- Do a final review for:
    - title length
    - page numbering
    - heading wording consistency
    - abstract word count
    - font and margin consistency
    - references formatting
    - appendix callouts in the body
    - AI disclosure, if applicable

## Minimum submission-critical checklist

1. Use the `Traditional Format` exactly as ordered.
2. Keep `12 pt font`, `1-inch margins`, double spacing, and left-justified text.
3. Hide page numbers on the `Title Page` and `Approval Page`.
4. Keep the `Abstract` under `350 words`.
5. Make table of contents entries match the body text exactly.
6. Include AI disclosure and citation if AI was used.
7. Meet defense and final PDF submission deadlines.
